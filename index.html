We are Natural Honey — a proudly women-owned honey farm nestled in the green farmlands of Mustafapur, Patna, Bihar. Our bees roam freely through wildflower meadows and mustard fields, crafting honey as pure as nature intended.

Unlike mass-produced brands, we never add sugar, colors, or preservatives. What you taste is exactly what our bees made — nothing more, nothing less.
